# Permit signing — USDC

End-to-end EIP-2612 flow against USDC on mainnet. Reads
`DOMAIN_SEPARATOR` + `nonces(owner)` via multicall, builds
the typed `Permit` struct, and asks the wallet to sign it
via `eth_signTypedData_v4`. The returned 65-byte signature
is what a third party (a relayer, a router, a contract's
`permitAndCall`) needs to spend tokens on your behalf — no
prior `approve()` transaction.
