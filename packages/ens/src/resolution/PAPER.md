# ENS resolution orchestration

Not a numbered standard. Composes EIP-137 (Registry + Addr
Resolver), EIP-181 (reverse `name()`), EIP-2304 (multicoin
`addr()`), EIP-634 (text records), ENSIP-12 (avatar parsing),
and ENSIP-15 (normalization) into the forward/reverse lookup
flows dapps actually want.
